# Article associations and current visibility

At most20Product and20Concern relationships per revision, no duplicate ID, each explicit order0..999999999 then UUID. New selection requires visible/unarchived Product and Brand, or active/unarchived Concern. Product availability unavailable/on_request is allowed: an editorial link is not a purchase/clinical decision. No Variant choice, price/image inheritance, Product recommendation mutation or automatic keyword matching.

Revision children preserve membership/order across draft changes. Existing now-hidden links may remain in drafts and subsequent publications; public projections suppress them. Restoration of current Catalog visibility restores a saved link without silently adding any new membership. No fallback hidden name/slug/image leaks. Related Products expose only id/name/slug/declared availability; related Concerns id/name/slug. Names/slugs come from current visible Catalog, while Article body/title remain immutable publication content. No full Product hydration, prices, credentials, source IDs or internal audit fields serialize.

Category0..1 is optional, not a Concern. A newly selected category must be active/unarchived. Category label/description is snapshotted in Article revisions and refreshed by publication; live state gates whether category data may appear. Hiding/archive leaves Article discoverable without category data, and category-scoped listing404. Restore hidden. No default category, hard deletion, hierarchy or multi-category Article.

Article detail batches current Catalog queries within the read snapshot. Concern filtering resolves a currently visible Concern before membership filtering. Public list contains no related-entity metadata; it cannot redisclose hidden links. Unknown/hidden filter entity404 safely, no draft associations used. Concurrent Catalog changes after snapshot admission do not retract an in-flight response. URL strings manually authored in text are not automatic Catalog relationships and do not modify Catalog; link syntax acceptance is not remote verification.
