# Delivery and future confirmed Orders

Implemented6H: published Zone identity/revision/name/governorate/optional city, independent nullable USD/SYP informational fees, explicit publication and private contacts. No location inference, selected Zone persistence, binding quote or worker assignment. Checkout Customer contact and Gift recipient remain separate immutable preliminary facts, with unchanged fingerprints and committed response replay. They do not depend on the delivery reader or worker schema.

Existing normalized geographic uniqueness applies to the current Settings row, not every historical published label. Editing geography in a draft can retain its old published location while another Zone uses that location in its current row. Labels therefore are not public identities or lookup authority; UUID and exact publication version are authoritative.6H does not pick a fee by comparing label text or introduce a new overlap rule. Any future selected final delivery/coverage rule belongs to the6I gate.

Future6I must first obtain its own commercial design approval. It may consume Settings' authoritative published Zone projection and current private Worker identity; do not duplicate Zones, import public display as a binding quote, or trust a client-supplied fee. Establish selected identity/version, coverage/address confirmation, current archival/publication state, currency-specific fee availability and consent at the defined confirmation boundary. Null is unknown, zero explicitly configured free, both currencies independent, no conversion. Current6H fees have not been agreed as final charges.

6I decides immutable confirmed-Order delivery/contact/fee/currency snapshot and attribution, payable total, deposit/full payment and manual receipt verification, fee changes between review and confirmation, optional active worker assignment, reassignment/history and fulfillment transitions. Those are future design responsibilities, not implemented behavior or authorization to ship. A disabled worker retains identity but has no current6H Order references. Current schema intentionally avoids assignment FKs or future shipment aggregates.

PAY-001 remains authoritative: independently verify required payment/approved deposit before confirmed Order or inventory allocation, then recheck fulfillment; no invented zero-payment verification or automatic COD exception. Do not reinterpret preliminary historical addresses/prices through today's Zone revisions, overwrite a Gift recipient with account identity, or let publication/review completion trigger dispatch. No financial or fulfillment code is scaffolded here.
